Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 9, 2026

Ommy Games ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website ommygames.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our contact form:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Service interest (selected from dropdown)
  • Message content

We do not use cookies for tracking or analytics on this website. No personal data is automatically collected through browsing.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our contact form is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Discuss potential project collaborations
  • Provide requested information about our services

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Our contact form is processed by FormSubmit.co, a form submission service. When you submit the form, your data is transmitted through their servers to our email. Please review FormSubmit's privacy policy for details on their data handling.

4. Data Retention

Form submissions are delivered to our email inbox. We retain correspondence for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected or as required by applicable law.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@ommygames.com.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
