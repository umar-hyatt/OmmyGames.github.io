Our Story

Welcome to Ommy Games

Founded in 2020 by Umar Hyatt, Ommy Games was built with a vision to craft immersive and innovative gaming experiences that captivate players worldwide.

Starting as a passionate indie team, we have grown into a 10-person studio specializing in mobile and VR game development.

With a commitment to creativity and cutting-edge technology, we strive to push the boundaries of interactive entertainment, bringing unique and engaging games to players across the globe.
