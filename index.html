Our Story

Building Games That
Players Remember

What started in 2020 as a one-person passion project by Umar Hyatt has grown into a full-service game studio with a 10-person team, 100+ completed projects, and players across the globe.

We specialize in mobile and VR game development, shipping titles across Android, iOS, and Meta Quest. But we don't just build our own games — we help clients bring their visions to life through full-cycle development, marketing collaboration, and live ops support.

What We Offer

Services & Collaboration

Beyond our own titles, we partner with clients to build, maintain, and grow games across every platform.

Full-Cycle Game Development

From concept art to store submission — we handle the entire pipeline. Share your idea and we'll turn it into a polished, market-ready game.

Marketing & UA Collaboration

ASO, creative ad production, user acquisition strategy, and A/B testing — we help maximize your game's reach and retention.

Game Maintenance & Live Ops

Keep your live game healthy — bug fixes, SDK updates, OS compatibility patches, new content drops, and ongoing performance optimization.

Cross-Platform Porting

Bring your game to new audiences. We port between Android, iOS, WebGL, and Meta Quest VR with optimized performance on each platform.

QA & Testing

Comprehensive quality assurance — device testing, performance profiling, crash analytics, and automated regression testing for rock-solid releases.

Game Design Consulting

Expert guidance on game mechanics, monetization strategy, player psychology, and market positioning to set your project up for success.
